Apex Engineering College - Hostel and Accommodation 2024-25

HOSTEL FACILITIES:
We provide on-campus accommodation facilities for outstation students. There are separate hostels for boys and girls with basic amenities to ensure a comfortable stay.

BOYS HOSTEL:
- Total Capacity: 400 Students
- Room Type: Double/Triple Occupancy (Non-AC)
- Facilities: Common room with TV, Wi-Fi access (limited bandwidth), basic gym, indoor games, and round-the-clock security.

GIRLS HOSTEL:
- Total Capacity: 300 Students
- Room Type: Double/Triple Occupancy (Non-AC)
- Facilities: Common room, Wi-Fi access, strict security protocols, and indoor table tennis.

MESS FACILITIES:
- A common dining hall serving vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals.
- Menu is decided by the student committee on a monthly basis.

FEE STRUCTURE (Per Semester):
- Accommodation Charges (Double Occupancy): ₹50,000
- Mess Charges: ₹30,000
- Total Hostel Fee per semester: ₹80,000

Note: Allocation of hostel rooms is strictly on a first-come, first-served basis.
