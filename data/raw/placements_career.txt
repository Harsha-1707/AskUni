Anurag University - Training and Placements 2024-25

TRAINING AND PLACEMENT CELL:
Our Training and Placement (T&P) Cell works dedicatedly to ensure students get placed in decent companies. Being an average engineering college, our focus is on bringing a wide range of mass recruiters and mid-tier product companies to campus.

PLACEMENT STATISTICS (2023-24):
- Total Students Eligible: 850
- Students Placed: 650
- Placement Percentage: ~76%
- Highest Salary Package: ₹12.00 LPA (Offered by a leading FinTech startup)
- Average Salary Package: ₹4.50 LPA
- Median Salary Package: ₹3.80 LPA

TOP RECRUITERS:
1. IT Services: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Tech Mahindra, HCL Technologies.
2. Core Engineering: L&T Construction, Tata Motors, Bosch.
3. Emerging Startups.

PRE-PLACEMENT TRAINING:
To bridge the gap between academic curriculum and industry expectations, we offer:
- Aptitude and Soft Skills training from 2nd year onwards.
- Technical coding rounds practice (C, C++, Java, Python).
- Mock Interviews and Group Discussions.
- Mandatory 6-week summer internship after the 6th semester.

ENTREPRENEURSHIP CELL:
We also encourage students to start their own ventures. The E-Cell provides basic incubation facilities, mentorship from alumni, and networking opportunities.
