Anurag University - Admissions Policy 2024-25

ADMISSION CRITERIA FOR B.TECH:
1. Academic Qualifications: Candidates must have passed 10+2 (or equivalent) examinations from a recognized board with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with one of Chemistry/Biotechnology/Biology/Technical Vocational subject.
2. Minimum Marks: A minimum of 60% aggregate marks in the above subjects (55% for SC/ST category candidates).
3. Entrance Examination: Admissions are primarily based on performance in national or state-level engineering entrance exams (like JEE Main or state CET). A valid scorecard is mandatory.
4. Management Quota: 20% of the seats are reserved for the management quota, for which direct applications can be made at the college admission office. Academic fee remains ₹2.58 Lakhs per year.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
- Step 1: Register online on the college portal (www.anurag.edu.in/admissions).
- Step 2: Fill out the detailed application form and upload necessary documents (10th & 12th mark sheets, entrance exam scorecard, ID proof).
- Step 3: Pay the application fee of ₹1,000.
- Step 4: Shortlisted candidates will be called for counseling.

COUNSELING & SEAT ALLOTMENT:
Seats are allotted based on merit, availability, and choices filled by the candidates during the counseling session.

Document Verification: Original documents are verified at the time of reporting to the college.
