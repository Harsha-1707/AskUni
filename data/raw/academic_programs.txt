Apex Engineering College - Academic Programs & General Info 2024-25

UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMS (B.Tech) - 4 YEARS
We offer the following core engineering branches:
1. Computer Science & Engineering
2. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
3. Electronics & Communication Engineering
4. Mechanical Engineering
5. Civil Engineering

All B.Tech programs have a total of 160 credits over 4 years.

PLACEMENTS:
As an average engineering college, we aim to provide decent placement opportunities to all our eligible students.
- Average Package: ₹4.5 LPA
- Highest Package: ₹12 LPA
- Top Recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Tech Mahindra

ACADEMIC CALENDAR:
Semester System (2 semesters per year)
- Odd Semester: August to December
- Even Semester: January to May

ATTENDANCE POLICY:
- Minimum 75% attendance is strictly mandatory for appearing in the end-semester examinations.
- Medical leaves are considered up to 10% relaxation, making the minimum requirement 65% in case of severe medical issues with proper documentation.

FACILITIES:
- Central Library with over 30,000 books and access to digital resources.
- Well-equipped laboratories for all core engineering branches.
- Sports complex including a cricket ground, basketball court, and indoor games.
- Campus-wide Wi-Fi connectivity.
